About Monitor Consults

Founded in 2021 at the height of the COVID-19 pandemic as a bold response to uncertainty

Our Story

Founded in 2021 at the height of the COVID-19 pandemic, Monitor Consults began as a bold response to uncertainty—helping organizations and businesses adapt, survive, and thrive in rapidly changing environments.

What started as a solo freelance consultancy has grown into a trusted partner for global clients seeking clarity, structure, and success in their projects.

What We Do

We help organizations and businesses turn ideas into actionable, results-driven projects. Whether you're launching a new initiative, scaling impact, or strengthening internal systems, we bring the structure, strategy, and support you need to succeed.

Meet the Team

Esther M.

Founder & Lead Consultant

Esther Muita is the driving force behind Monitor Consults, a consultancy born out of a belief that great ideas deserve flawless execution and measurable results.

With a solid background in Development Studies; a Masters of Science in Project Management and Professional Certification in Monitoring & Evaluation, she blends strategy, structure, and insight to drive results.

Prior Work & Projects

Evidence of impact across health, governance, economic empowerment, and research

Asset and Liability Management Policy

Kenya Roads Board

Authored the Asset and Liability Management Policy at Kenya Roads Board and restructured the financial standard operating procedures.

Stakeholders: Kenya Roads Board Assets and Finance Departments

Government to Government Transition Monitoring

USAID | Vukisha 95 & Dhibiti Projects

Monitored the Government to Government transition of the USAID donor support from non-profits to the County Fund and the impact on health facilities in Kenya, especially in HIV programming.

Stakeholders: County Governments in Homabay, Kisii, Kiambu, Kirinyaga, Muranga and Nairobi County, USAID, Dhibiti, LVCT Health

Mental Health Integration & Training

University of Maryland School of Medicine | LVCT Health

Trained over 100+ health workers in Nairobi and Kiambu as co-researchers to identify mental health gaps and explore ways to integrate mental health into mainstream healthcare. Designed human-centered project approach using CBPR tools—such as storytelling, focus group discussions, photovoice, and body mapping—increasing healthcare access among youth by 85%.

Stakeholders: University of Maryland School of Medicine, LVCT Health, Liverpool School of Tropical Medicine (LSTM)
View Project

Female Genital Schistosomiasis Awareness

Community Health Campaign

Created campaign tools to support awareness surrounding Female Genital Schistosomiasis (FGS) that disproportionately affects women and girls who lack access to safe water, sanitation, and quality healthcare. Addressed misdiagnosis, stigma, discrimination, and gender-based violence (GBV).

Focus: Healthcare worker training, community awareness, stigma reduction
View Project

HIV/TB Reduction in Prison Systems

CDC | LVCT Health

Conducted data collection and research in 5 prisons to support the implementation of a 5-year HIV/TB reduction project within the Kenyan prison system, leading a team across Central Kenya. Ensured inclusive engagement by addressing unique health needs of marginalized prison populations, aligning efforts with DEI principles.

Stakeholders: Government of Kenya, Center for Disease Control and Prevention, LVCT Health
View Project

Business Development & Quality Assurance

USAID | Society for Women and AIDS in Kenya

Monitored over 150 businesses for quality assurance after training and offering business startup kits and financial linkages for women and youth. Facilitated improvement of livelihood for over 80+ households in Kenya and promoted financial inclusion.

Stakeholders: USAID, Society for Women and AIDS in Kenya, Goose Hill Consults Ltd, Jumia Kenya
